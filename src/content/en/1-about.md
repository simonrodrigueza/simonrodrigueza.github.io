I am a Colombian astronomy student focused on protoplanetary disks. I want to understand how their chemistry evolves, and how extreme environments shape their structure and, ultimately, the formation of planets. To do this, I run thermochemical simulations and generate synthetic spectra to build and test theories of disk structure and evolution.
I am also interested in how the dust distribution creates traps that help disks hold their structure for longer.
