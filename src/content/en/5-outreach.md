### RECA Mentorship Program

*Member of the Mentorship Committee since September 2025.*

[RECA](https://www.astroreca.org), the Colombian Network of Astronomy Students, supports students and early-career astronomers. Since 2020, its [Mentorship Program](https://www.astroreca.org/mentoring) has connected undergraduates in Colombia with more advanced astronomers, who advise them as they plan their careers: graduate opportunities in Colombia and abroad, preparing CVs, motivation letters and exams, choosing universities, scholarships and funding, and adapting to a new country and to graduate academic life. In 2026–2027 the program runs its sixth edition.

On the committee, I manage communication logistics and track attendance at panels and other program activities. I also analyze data from participation forms to produce statistics that help us measure the program's impact.

At the IX Colombian Congress of Astronomy and Astrophysics (COCOA 2026) in Bogotá, I presented RECA together with other members of the network, giving an overview of its activities with a focus on the Mentorship Program.
