### A JVLA search for neutral hydrogen in a dark galaxy candidate

*Mock observing proposal, Radio Astronomy course, Universidad de Antioquia, 2026.*

Dark galaxies, low-mass dark-matter halos that failed to form stars, could still hold rotating gas detectable through the 21 cm line. I wrote a full-format JVLA proposal to map the HI distribution and kinematics of J111803.15+311514.4, one of 70 dark galaxy candidates selected from the FASHI survey, observing at 1420 MHz in B configuration (~5″) to balance resolution and sensitivity. It covers the science case, target selection, and the instrument configuration and sensitivity justification.

[Read the proposal (PDF, in Spanish) ↗](/projects/jvla-dark-galaxy-proposal.pdf)

### Inspecting, flagging and imaging VLA data with CASA

*Course project, Radio Astronomy, Universidad de Antioquia, 2026. CASA, Python.*

Using EVLA observations of the supernova remnant 3C 391, I worked through the interferometric workflow in CASA: inspecting the visibilities and uv-coverage, flagging bad data, and imaging with tclean. I compared dirty and CLEAN images of the calibrator and the target, and how natural, uniform and Briggs weighting trade sensitivity for resolution.

[View the notebook (PDF, in Spanish) ↗](/projects/casa-3c391.pdf)

### Methanol rotational diagram of a massive young stellar object

*Course project, Astrochemistry, Universidad de Antioquia, 2025. Python.*

Starting from an ALMA Band 6 spectrum of the massive young stellar object G050.2213-00.6063, I built a rotational diagram of methanol to derive its excitation temperature. I then used the CDMS partition function to estimate the methanol column density.

### Particle orbits around rotating bodies: geodesics in the Kerr metric

*Course project, Relativity and Gravitation, 2025. Python, EinsteinPy.*

The Kerr metric describes spacetime around rotating bodies, from stars to black holes. I computed its Christoffel symbols with EinsteinPy and wrote my own numerical solver for the geodesic equations, first in the slow-rotation limit and then for the full metric. With it I explored equatorial, polar, prograde and retrograde orbits of test particles.
